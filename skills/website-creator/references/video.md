## Selection and method

Research starts from the user's three supplied videos, then follows authors, description links, and related 2026 tutorials and app critiques. It prioritizes recent months, clearly shown results, and distinct procedures. January courses supplement technical details; some less popular videos provide a concrete variant or primary UX guidance.

Automatic transcripts locate passages; names and availability are checked against descriptions and official sources. Frames were observed in Chrome with playback muted. Long courses have explicitly selective reading in their entries. The atlas stores static examples; implementation behavior is tested in the website being built.

App-analysis additions have complete available transcript reviews. U01–U22 collect demonstrated tasks, adapted psychological explanations, and checks for the resulting behavior. Their evidence boundaries and free reference route are in [User experience recipes](user-experience.md). B01–B06 add [Blender workflows](blender.md); P11–P12 add product demonstrations.

The subsequent expansion follows actual related-video and homepage recommendations observed in Chrome. Ducky 3D's related list led to Sajid's DBJoepxxQdg and Higgsfield's OiULPvTJ-0E. The homepage supplied ihWJtNaLYLo and the sphere short q1QQN08ZK6I. Each candidate was then checked for date, source, and a distinct procedure; the recommendation itself is a discovery signal.

Sale prices, exceptional build times, and ranking promises remain in original video titles for identification. Recipe selection follows the demonstrated procedure. Codex prompts, result checks, and combinations M01–M16 are original collection adaptations.
