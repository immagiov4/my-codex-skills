# Combinations by website type

These are original collection adaptations. Each ingredient links to a recipe with its source. Choose by content and requested result; a combination becomes a reusable template after testing in the project. For a new or replacement identity, [choose the visual direction](design-direction.md) first and use these combinations for content and interaction needs.

## M01

### Photographic product page

Inputs: A product photo, description, price or contact request.

Structure: Open with the object and heading; add benefits and specifications in the same grid.

Recipes: [P09](procedure.md#p09), [P02](procedure.md#p02), [C14](componenti-css.md#c14), [C15](componenti-css.md#c15).

Free route: Your own image and HTML/CSS.

Variant: S01 for a narrated disassembly; T04 for tilt and product selection.

## M02

### A product that comes apart

Inputs: Matching intact/exploded views or your own 3D model.

Structure: Opening section, disassembly, details, final action.

Recipes: [P01](procedure.md#p01), [P02](procedure.md#p02), [S01](scorrimento.md#s01), [C14](componenti-css.md#c14).

Free route: Images and CSS transitions, or your own Three.js model.

Variant: Optional video with a selected model and spending cap.

## M03

### Café or restaurant

Inputs: Authorized venue photos, menu, and contact details.

Structure: Make the venue, cuisine, menu, and visit information findable in the chosen composition. Let actual photographs, lettering, atmosphere, and the visitor's decision determine the opening and section rhythm.

Recipes: [P01](procedure.md#p01) for references and [P10](procedure.md#p10) for retained content. Choose [S03](scorrimento.md#s03) for a growing photographic window, [S04](scorrimento.md#s04) for a justified horizontal sequence, or [C08](componenti-css.md#c08) for contained footer motion when the direction benefits from it.

Free route: Your own photos, CSS, and native scrolling.

Variant: Authorized stock clip for the moving window; GSAP for horizontal panels.

## M04
